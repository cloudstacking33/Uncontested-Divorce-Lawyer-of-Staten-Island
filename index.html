Our approach to uncontested divorce

An uncontested divorce works best when both spouses are honest about finances, open to compromise and willing to put the details in writing. As your uncontested divorce lawyer, we turn those agreements into a precise settlement and the paperwork a New York court expects, and we explain every document in plain language.

We do not push conflict where none exists. Many clients choose this route because it usually costs less than a trial, keeps personal details out of a public fight and gives both people more control over the result.

When a divorce is not simple

Not every case stays uncontested. A spouse may refuse to sign, live overseas or be impossible to locate. Long separations are common, but New York has no automatic divorce after time apart, so the marriage stays in place until a court ends it. If a case becomes a dispute, we advise you as a contested divorce lawyer and explain the route before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

What is an uncontested divorce?

Both spouses agree to end the marriage and agree on the terms. Nobody argues in front of a judge, and the process is mostly paperwork.

How long does an uncontested divorce take?

Part of the timeline is yours to control and part belongs to the court. Anyone promising a specific date is guessing.

Can we stay uncontested if we have children?

Yes. There are more terms to agree on, and the court looks harder at what you have agreed.

What if my spouse will not sign?

Refusing to sign does not stop a divorce in New York. It changes the route and usually makes the case slower.

Do I need a lawyer for an amicable divorce?

Not always, but a divorce attorney helps make sure the agreement is complete, accurate and enforceable.

Can my spouse be overseas?

Yes, provided New York is the right place to file. The complications involve jurisdiction and how papers are delivered.

No Fault and Simple Divorce Lawyer

New York allows a no fault divorce, which means you do not need to prove that either spouse did something wrong. A simple divorce lawyer helps you file on that basis and keeps the focus on the practical terms instead of blame. This is a good fit for couples who want a quick divorce attorney and a calm process. We review the grounds, confirm eligibility, prepare the forms and make sure your agreement addresses finances, property and any children. Keeping the process free of accusations usually saves time, money and stress, and it makes it easier to stay on good terms after the marriage ends.
